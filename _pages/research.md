My research is in **scientific machine learning**, with a focus on **operator learning**, **kernel methods**, and **neural operators** for problems involving differential equations.

I am interested in building accurate, efficient, and stable models that learn mappings between function spaces. My work connects ideas from classical approximation theory, numerical analysis, kernel methods, and modern deep learning.

## Research Interests

- Scientific Machine Learning
- Operator Learning
- Fourier Neural Operators
- DeepONets
- Kernel Methods
- Numerical Methods for Partial Differential Equations
- High-Performance Computing

## Current Research Directions

### Adaptive Kernel Expansion DeepONet

I am developing adaptive kernel-based neural operator architectures that combine neural networks with kernel approximation ideas. The goal is to improve flexibility, stability, and generalization in operator learning.

### Barycentric DeepONet

I am studying barycentric formulations of DeepONets for learning solution operators. This direction explores how barycentric representations can be used to improve the structure and accuracy of neural operator models.

### Fourier Neural Operators

I work with Fourier Neural Operators for learning mappings between functions, especially in problems involving partial differential equations. I am interested in how spectral representations affect accuracy, discretization, and generalization.

### Query-Based Neural Operators

I am exploring neural operator architectures that can evaluate solutions directly at query locations, rather than only on fixed output grids.

## Broader Goals

My long-term research goal is to develop reliable scientific machine learning methods that can be used for computational science, engineering, and applied mathematics problems.
