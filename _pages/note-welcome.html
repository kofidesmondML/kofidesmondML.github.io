Welcome to my research notes

I am using this space to keep a short, informal record of concepts, questions, papers, and computational experiments that arise in my work as a computational mathematics researcher.

My interests cross several connected areas—including kernel approximation, numerical analysis, scientific machine learning, operator learning, and partial differential equations. Each area has its own notation and terminology. Writing through those connections helps me clarify what I understand, identify what I still need to learn, and communicate mathematical ideas more clearly.

The notes may include explanations of foundational terminology, summaries of papers, small numerical experiments, implementation lessons, and open questions. They should be read as working documents rather than finished research claims. Preliminary or collaborative results will only be discussed when they are ready to be shared publicly.
