I am **Desmond Kofi Boateng**, a Ph.D. student in **Computing with a Data Science emphasis** at **Boise State University**, based in the School of Computing.

My research interests are in **scientific machine learning**, **operator learning**, **kernel methods**, **Fourier neural operators**, **DeepONets**, and numerical methods for problems involving partial differential equations. I am especially interested in building efficient and accurate machine learning models for learning mappings between function spaces.

My current work explores operator learning architectures such as **DeepONets**, **Fourier Neural Operators**, **barycentric DeepONets**, and **adaptive kernel-based neural operators**. I am interested in how classical approximation theory, kernel methods, and modern neural networks can be combined to improve generalization, stability, and interpretability in scientific machine learning.
