AKED & TreeAKED

Adaptive Kernel Expansion DeepONets (AKED) investigate whether the learned representation in a DeepONet can respond more directly to the geometry and resolution of function samples.

The work enriches the trunk representation with trainable kernel expansions, then evaluates how those choices affect accuracy and generalization across computational grids. TreeAKED is an ongoing hierarchical extension of this direction, organizing kernel expansions to support richer multiscale structure.

Polyharmonic Splines for Operator Learning

This project studies polyharmonic splines as interpretable kernel bases for operator-learning architectures.

Polyharmonic splines bring a classical approximation-theory perspective to scientific machine learning. Their structure can support discretization-aware function representations while avoiding the need to preselect a shape parameter. I presented this work at the SIAM Pacific Northwest Biennial Meeting and at CBMS-AMML in 2025.

  • Connects classical kernel approximation with neural operator models.
  • Studies accuracy across multiple PDE benchmarks and sample grids.
  • Uses reproducible GPU experiments to compare architectural choices.
